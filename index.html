CustomEye

Parametric eyewear design for Autodesk Fusion

CustomEye is an add-in for Autodesk Fusion designed to assist eyewear designers and makers in creating and modifying parametric eyeglass frame designs.

About CustomEye

CustomEye provides dedicated tools for controlling eyewear geometry and design parameters directly inside Autodesk Fusion. Its purpose is to make the creation and adjustment of custom frame designs faster and more consistent.

Compatibility

CustomEye is developed for Autodesk Fusion. Compatibility information for each published version is provided on the Autodesk Design and Make Marketplace listing.

Support

For support, questions, or feedback about CustomEye, please use the support contact information provided on the CustomEye listing in the Autodesk Design and Make Marketplace.

Privacy
