Privacy Policy

CustomEye – Autodesk Fusion Add-in
Last updated: September 3, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how the CustomEye add-in for Autodesk Fusion handles information when you use the software.

2. Personal Data

CustomEye does not collect, store, sell, share, or transmit personal data.

3. Design and Fusion Data

CustomEye operates within Autodesk Fusion and processes the design information required for its functions locally within the user's Fusion environment. CustomEye does not independently transmit Fusion documents, design geometry, project information, or other user-created content to the publisher.

4. Analytics and Tracking

CustomEye does not use advertising trackers, third-party analytics services, or behavioral tracking technologies.

5. External Services

CustomEye does not require a publisher-operated online account or cloud service for its core functionality. Autodesk Fusion itself may process information according to Autodesk's own terms and privacy policies. Such processing is controlled by Autodesk and is separate from CustomEye.

6. Children's Privacy

CustomEye does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from any other category of user.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the functionality of CustomEye changes. The current version will be made available at this page, with the revision date shown above.

8. Contact

For privacy questions concerning CustomEye, please use the publisher contact information provided on the CustomEye listing in the Autodesk Design and Make Marketplace.

CustomEye is an independent third-party add-in for Autodesk Fusion. Autodesk and Fusion are trademarks of Autodesk, Inc.
